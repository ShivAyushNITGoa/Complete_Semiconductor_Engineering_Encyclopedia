# Energy Bands

## Prerequisites
- Atomic structure
- Quantum physics
- Solid state physics

## Concept
In solids, individual atomic energy levels combine to form energy bands.

Important bands:

- Valence band
- Conduction band
- Forbidden energy gap

## Band Gap

Band gap determines semiconductor behavior.

Examples:

Silicon:
- Band gap approximately 1.12 eV

Gallium Nitride:
- Wide band gap semiconductor

## Importance
Band structure determines:

- Conductivity
- Optical properties
- Temperature behavior
- Device applications

## Applications

Used in:

- Transistors
- LEDs
- Solar cells
- Power electronics
